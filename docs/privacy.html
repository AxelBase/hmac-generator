Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2025

1. Overview

The HMAC-SHA256 Generator is a cryptographic tool created by AxelBase to help developers securely generate HMAC hashes in a simple, browser-based interface. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled while using the utility. Because the tool is built on a zero-logging, zero-storage, and 100% client-side architecture, your data never leaves your device.

2. What We Do Not Collect

To maintain complete user privacy, we do not collect or store any of the following:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, location, or IP address.
  • Cryptographic inputs including secret keys, messages, imported files, or generated hashes.
  • Analytics or usage data; we do not run tracking scripts, ad pixels, or cookies.
  • Device or browser data beyond what your browser already handles locally.

Every operation—secret key parsing, message encoding, hash generation, and file imports—happens entirely inside your browser via JavaScript. No data is transmitted to any server.

3. How the Tool Operates

The HMAC-SHA256 Generator uses modern browser cryptography APIs to create hashes. All logic runs client-side, and the tool does not communicate with external services. Any files you import are processed in memory only, and are immediately discarded when you close or refresh the page.

4. Third-Party Services

The tool does not integrate analytics, ads, remote scripts, or any third-party collectors. Open-source libraries that may exist in the bundle run locally and do not transmit data externally.

5. Cookies

The HMAC-SHA256 Generator uses no cookies whatsoever. It does not maintain sessions, track returning users, or store preferences.

6. Security Recommendations

Although we do not collect or access your data, the protection of your secret keys depends on your own environment. We recommend using updated browsers, avoiding public/shared devices, and clearing sensitive text after use.

7. Children’s Privacy

This tool is intended for developers and technical users. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect improvements or clarifications. The effective date above will always indicate the latest revision.

By using the HMAC-SHA256 Generator, you acknowledge and agree to this Privacy Policy. Because the tool collects nothing, your data remains yours—and yours alone.
